Pricebook is the price book frugal shoppers have always kept in a notebook, rebuilt for the phone in your hand in the aisle.

Point it at a barcode, type the price on the shelf tag, and it tells you straight away: Stock up, Good price, Normal, Pricey, or Fake sale. Every verdict comes from your own history, at the stores you actually use, not from ads or a price feed.

SCAN IN THE AISLE
• Reads the barcode with the camera, even on a crowded shelf
• The first time, tell it what the item is. After that the barcode opens it instantly
• A rubber-stamp verdict, the reasons behind it, and a gauge showing where this price sits between your best and worst
• If another store usually has it for less, it says which one and by how much

THE PRICE BOOK
• Every item with its price history at every store, drawn as a chart with the sales marked
• Your best, usual and worst price per ounce, pound, litre or each
• Where it is cheapest, and what switching stores would save you in a year at your pace
• How often it goes on sale, and how many to buy to last until the next one

WHICH IS CHEAPER?
• Compare two to six sizes and deals: multi-packs, 2 for $5, buy one get one, percent off, coupons
• Weight, volume and count are converted for you
• The best buy is marked, with what the others cost extra

A SMARTER LIST
• Add what you need and the list sorts itself by the store where each thing is usually cheapest
• A trip total, and how much splitting the trip saves against the cheapest single store
• Skip a store this week with one tap and the list rearranges

Stores, aisles and units are yours to change. Export every price you have logged as a spreadsheet.

No account, no ads, no tracking, no subscription. Your prices stay on your phone.
